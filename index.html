Hello! Welcome to the English Deaf Corpus.

My name is Zulma Santiago. I am a Deaf faculty member at the University of Florida and a collaborator on this research project. Our goal is to collect authentic English writing from diverse Deaf and Hard of Hearing adults. Your writing can help researchers and educators better understand and value the different ways our communities use English—not as deficits, but as linguistic diversity.

You may participate if you are 18 or older, identify as Deaf or Hard of Hearing, and can complete the study in English. Participation is online and includes a short background questionnaire and one to six writing prompts.

Participation is voluntary, and your information will be anonymized and stored securely.

01 / THE PROJECT

Real writing.
More representation.

A corpus is an organized collection of texts. Ours brings together authentic English writing produced by Deaf and Hard of Hearing adults.

Many ideas about writing have been developed without enough representation from Deaf and Hard of Hearing communities. This University of Florida project seeks to help address that gap.

By sharing their writing, participants can help researchers and educators develop a more complete and inclusive understanding of English writing—recognizing linguistic diversity rather than treating differences as deficits.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

A few things to know.

Is participation voluntary?

Yes. You may decline to participate or withdraw your consent at any time without consequences.

Is there payment for participating?

There is no direct financial benefit or payment for participating in this study.

How will my information be used?

Your written responses and background questionnaire information will be anonymized and stored securely in a password-protected University of Florida Dropbox folder accessible only to the research team. Anonymized texts and survey responses may be used in future research or shared with researchers whose work has received appropriate Institutional Review Board approval.

Can I share this study with my community?

Yes. You can share the study link or the recruitment poster through your organization or community networks. Contact Angela if you would like help.
